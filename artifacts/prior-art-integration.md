# Prior-Art Integration: The Harmonic Paradigm as Synthesis of 5 QNFO Papers

## Executive Summary

Five existing QNFO papers independently explore different rungs of what this research program now recognizes as the **Harmonic Ladder** — an 8-rung logarithmic spine from the transmon (≈10⁻⁶ m, α ≈ 0) to quantum gravity (≈10⁻³⁵ m, α → 1), connected by a single dimensionless order parameter α measuring distance from pure harmonicity.

**No single existing paper connects these rungs.** The Harmonic Paradigm is not a competitor to these papers — it is their **integrating synthesis**, providing the ladder they individually lack. Each paper contributes essential prior art that the Harmonic Paradigm **builds upon, generalizes, and unifies**.

This document:
1. Maps each of the 5 papers to its rung(s) on the Harmonic Ladder
2. Identifies exactly what each paper contributes to the synthesis
3. Identifies what each paper lacks — the gap the synthesis fills
4. Positions the Harmonic Paradigm as the **single framework** that connects them all

## 1. The 5 Prior-Art Papers — Rung-by-Rung Mapping

### 1.1 Ultrametric Quantum Gravity and Computation

| Attribute | Detail |
|:----------|:-------|
| **DOI** | [10.5281/zenodo.19397516](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.19397516) |
| **Ladder Rung(s)** | 7 (p-adic oscillator), 8 (quantum gravity) |
| **α role** | None — no α parameter |

**What it contributes:**

- **p-adic harmonic oscillator:** The Bruhat-Tits tree provides a discrete, ultrametric state space whose spectrum is equally spaced in the p-adic norm — a form of discrete scale invariance.
- **Ultrametric Wheeler-DeWitt constraint:** Gravity reformulated as an ultrametric constraint on the configuration space rather than a dynamical field on a smooth manifold.
- **Passive geometric error correction:** The ultrametric geometry itself provides error correction — a theorem of the geometry, not an active protocol.
- **Bruhat-Tits tree as ladder:** The tree's level structure is naturally a ladder of equally-spaced scales — foreshadowing the Harmonic Ladder's equal ln(scale) spacing.

**What it lacks:**

- **No α parameter.** The distance from harmonicity is not quantified — the p-adic oscillator is treated as a standalone structure, not as one rung in a ladder connected by α.
- **No connection to transmon physics.** The p-adic oscillator and the transmon (a physical harmonic oscillator with measurable anharmonicity) are never linked.
- **No RG-harmonic isomorphism.** The renormalization group is not framed as the mechanism connecting the rungs.
- **No QED fine-structure constant connection.** α_EM is not identified as the same order parameter at a different rung.

**How the Harmonic Paradigm builds on it:**

The p-adic oscillator is rung 7 of the Harmonic Ladder. The equal spacing of the Bruhat-Tits tree levels is the ultrametric manifestation of the same discrete scale invariance that produces equally-spaced Efimov states (rung 2) and log-periodic CMB oscillations (CAL-03). The α → 1 limit of the universal β-function (rung 8) corresponds to the ultrametric closure described in this paper as "gravity as ultrametric constraint."

### 1.2 Fine-Structure Constant as a Cross-Ratio

| Attribute | Detail |
|:----------|:-------|
| **DOI** | [10.5281/zenodo.20108536](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.20108536) |
| **Ladder Rung(s)** | 6 (α-running, QED) |
| **α role** | Central — α is the main subject, but treated geometrically, not as a running β-function order parameter |

**What it contributes:**

- **Geometric reframing of α:** α_EM ≈ 1/137 is expressed as a cross-ratio of four projective points — a purely geometric object invariant under Möbius transformations.
- **Projective invariance:** The cross-ratio formulation makes α invariant under the conformal group, suggesting a deeper geometric origin.
- **Adelic extension (§7.7):** A brief extension to the adelic context, foreshadowing the role of Ostrowski's theorem in the full synthesis.
- **Why 1/137?:** The cross-ratio formulation provides a candidate answer — α is not an arbitrary parameter but a geometric invariant of a specific projective configuration.

**What it lacks:**

- **No transmon connection.** The geometric α is never linked to the transmon anharmonicity α_r — the Hamiltonian-level bridge is missing.
- **No β-function analysis.** α is treated as a static geometric object, not as a running coupling whose scale dependence encodes the RG flow connecting rungs.
- **No Harmonic Ladder.** The paper treats α in isolation — it does not place it on a ladder of equally-spaced scales.
- **No experimental calibration.** The cross-ratio formulation is mathematical, not calibrated against transmon or QED β-function data (CAL-01, CAL-02).

**How the Harmonic Paradigm builds on it:**

The cross-ratio formulation provides the **geometric foundation** for α at rung 6. The Harmonic Paradigm extends this by: (a) showing that α is NOT a static number but a running parameter α(μ), (b) connecting α at rung 6 (QED, α ≈ 1/128 at m_Z) to α at rung 0 (transmon, α_r ≈ 0) via the β-function, and (c) using the cross-ratio's projective invariance to argue that the 8-rung ladder is the maximal set of scales consistent with the symmetry.

### 1.3 Log-Periodic Oscillations in the CMB

| Attribute | Detail |
|:----------|:-------|
| **DOI** | (internal — no Zenodo DOI) |
| **Ladder Rung(s)** | Indirect test of 3 (SM GUT) and 8 (QG) |
| **α role** | None directly — tests discrete scale invariance, a consequence of the β-function structure |

**What it contributes:**

- **Falsifiable prediction:** Log-periodic oscillations in the CMB angular power spectrum C_ℓ, with period ln(q) ≈ ln(e) or ln(π), provide a direct experimental test of discrete scale invariance.
- **Methodology:** The log-periodogram technique for detecting equally-spaced oscillations in ln(ℓ) in CMB data.
- **CAL-03 calibration:** Defines a specific deadline (2028, CMB-S4) and falsification criterion (>3σ peak required).

**What it lacks:**

- **No connection to other rungs.** The CMB test is isolated — it probes discrete scale invariance but does not connect to the transmon, QED, or any other ladder rung.
- **No β-function formalism.** The log-periodic oscillations are hypothesized but not derived from a specific β-function (such as the β₀·α²·(1−α) form proposed in the rung selection criterion).
- **No α parameter.** The distance from harmonicity at the CMB scale is not quantified in terms of α.

**How the Harmonic Paradigm builds on it:**

The CMB log-periodic oscillations are a **direct consequence** of the discrete scale invariance implied by the Harmonic Ladder. If the β-function has a limit cycle (or if the equally-spaced ln(scale) structure is exact), then the CMB should show log-periodic features. The Harmonic Paradigm provides the **theoretical derivation** for the oscillations — from the β-function structure — rather than treating them as an independent hypothesis. CAL-03 is retained exactly as defined.

### 1.4 Adelic Synthesis: Pattern-Particle Correspondence

| Attribute | Detail |
|:----------|:-------|
| **DOI** | [10.5281/zenodo.21208568](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.21208568) |
| **Ladder Rung(s)** | 7 (p-adic) |
| **α role** | Implicit — the adelic product over all places (Archimedean + p-adic) is a form of ladder, but α is not the coordinate |

**What it contributes:**

- **Ostrowski's theorem:** The complete classification of absolute values on ℚ — exactly one Archimedean place (∞) and one p-adic place for each prime p.
- **Adelic anyons:** Quantum statistics that factorize across all completions of ℚ — a "place-by-place" factorization.
- **Full number-theoretic framework:** The adele ring 𝔸_ℚ as the natural arena for a theory that must be consistent across all scales.
- **Pattern-particle correspondence:** The claim that patterns (mathematical structures) and particles (physical objects) are the same thing, viewed at different places.

**What it lacks:**

- **No harmonic oscillator as universal grammar.** The paper is framed in the language of number theory and anyons, not harmonic oscillators. The connection to the physical harmonic oscillator (IR attractor) is missing.
- **No α as coordinate.** The adelic product runs over places, not over a single dimensionless parameter α.
- **No transmon connection.** The adelic framework is purely mathematical — it does not connect to the experimental reality of transmon circuits.
- **No β-function or RG framework.** The adelic places are static — there is no running, no flow, no scale dependence.

**How the Harmonic Paradigm builds on it:**

Ostrowski's theorem provides the **mathematical justification** for why the Harmonic Ladder has a discrete structure. The 8 rungs correspond to the 8 completions of ℚ (one Archimedean + seven p-adic) in the sense that each rung explores a different "place" in scale space. The Harmonic Paradigm adds: (a) the harmonic oscillator as the universal grammar across all places, (b) α as the single coordinate that runs from 0 (Archimedean, transmon) to 1 (ultrametric closure, QG), and (c) the β-function as the mechanism connecting the places.
